## Status meanings

- **A - automated coverage:** the actual implementation and mapped assertions support automated qualification. A report marks the case passed only after the complete matching-source pipeline and required separate public-package run succeed.
- **B - blocked:** the required authorized provider, physical device or hosted-authorization observation is absent. A deterministic fixture or emulated browser cannot satisfy it.
